Three core albums: Morning Garden, Moonlit Peace and What Remains Is Not Empty, plus Nature’s Rhythm.

What Remains Is Not Empty is an original song collection for people experiencing memory difficulties and their families, exploring everyday life, understanding and companionship.

The optional 40 Hz versions are recreational music, with no claim to improve memory or treat disease. Listen at a low volume and follow the in-app guidance.

Purpose and limits

Manmanlai is for recreation, education and everyday companionship. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent disease. Activity and listening records reflect participation, not medical assessments.

About Manmanlai

Manmanlai is an independent app project for older adults and families. We bring familiar everyday content, music and gentle interaction together in a clear, approachable experience.

The offline Android edition includes activity pages and accompanying audio, so built-in content can be used without internet access after installation.
