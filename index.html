慢慢来 Manmanlai

每天动动脑，生活更有趣

A little thinking. A little discovery. Every day.

一款为中老年人与家人设计的日常动脑、音乐欣赏、语言学习与生活陪伴 App。

An app for middle-aged and older adults and their families, bringing together everyday thinking activities, music, language learning and companionship.

记住几样物品，算一算买菜的钱，认一认钟表，听一首歌。《慢慢来》把观察、记忆、计算与判断融入熟悉的生活场景，让动脑成为日常生活中轻松、有趣的一部分。

Remember a few objects, work out the cost of groceries, read a clock or enjoy a song. Manmanlai brings observation, memory, everyday maths and decision-making into familiar situations, making mental engagement an enjoyable part of daily life.

不必赶时间，也不用和别人比较。可以慢慢看、慢慢想，选择自己喜欢的内容，享受每一次参与。

There is no need to rush or compare yourself with others. Take your time, choose what interests you and enjoy taking part.

音乐时光：有旋律，也有理解与陪伴 Music: melodies, understanding and companionship

《留下的不是空白》——为记忆渐渐模糊的朋友与家人创作的原创歌曲专辑

记性不如从前，并不意味着生活、情感和一个人的价值变成空白。从《我想告诉你》《慢一点，也没关系》到《留下的不是空白》，歌曲围绕日常生活、自我照顾与家人陪伴，表达被理解、被尊重与被爱的需要。

What Remains Is Not Empty — original songs created for people experiencing memory difficulties and their families

Changes in memory do not diminish a person’s life, feelings or worth. These songs explore everyday life, self-care and family companionship, giving voice to the need to be understood, respected and loved.

《晨光花园》——白天的温暖陪伴

温暖的钢琴与柔和弦乐，配以花园景致。可选择原音乐，或融合 40Hz 节律声音的版本；40Hz 节律版可用手机、平板或音箱低音量外放，无需耳机。

Morning Garden — a warm companion for daytime listening

Warm piano and gentle strings accompany garden scenes. Choose the original music or a version blended with a 40 Hz rhythmic sound. The rhythmic version can be played quietly through a phone, tablet or speakers, without headphones.

《月色安宁》——夜晚清醒时的安静欣赏

柔和的旋律与月夜画面，陪伴一天结束前的片刻。可选择原音乐，或融合 40Hz 双耳节拍的版本；双耳节拍版需使用左右声道分离的立体声耳机。

Moonlit Peace — quiet listening while awake in the evening

Gentle melodies and moonlit scenes accompany a quiet moment at the end of the day. Choose the original music or a version blended with a 40 Hz binaural beat. The binaural version requires stereo headphones with separate left and right channels.

此外，还有《大自然的节拍》，提供自然声音与音乐相融的两种 40Hz 聆听方式。喜欢的音乐可以加入收藏或自选专辑，Android 离线版支持连续播放与定时关闭。

The app also includes Nature’s Rhythm, blending nature sounds and music with two 40 Hz listening options. Save favourites or make personal playlists. The offline Android edition supports continuous playback and a stop timer.

40Hz 音乐属于可选的休闲欣赏内容，不宣称改善记忆、预防失智或治疗疾病。请以舒适的低音量聆听，并遵循应用内的使用说明。

The optional 40 Hz versions are for recreational listening, with no claim to improve memory or prevent or treat dementia or other conditions. Listen at a comfortable, low volume and follow the guidance in the app.

熟悉的生活，多样的动脑活动 Familiar situations, varied thinking activities

听力、专注与思考练习

听一听，再选字、选图或按提示完成小任务。结合翻牌配对、影子猜猜看、图像找找看与看图说话，让使用者在观察、记住线索、理解提示和作出选择的过程中，练习专注与思考。可以按需要重听，按自己的节奏参与。

Listening, attention and thinking activities

Listen, then choose a character or picture, or follow an everyday instruction. Matching pairs, silhouettes, picture searches and picture-based expression invite users to notice details, remember clues, understand prompts and make choices. Replay when needed and take part at your own pace.

数字、时钟与生活判断

认识数字、数量配对、一位数与两位数加减法；在《我的菜篮子》中选食材、算菜钱、付款找零，也在生活场景中认时钟、买早餐、辨方向，让计算与判断和日常生活联系起来。

Numbers, clocks and everyday choices

Match numbers and quantities, practise single- and two-digit addition and subtraction, and work out shopping costs, payments and change in My Grocery Basket. Explore clocks, breakfast shopping and directions in familiar scenes, connecting maths and decision-making with everyday life.

拼图、棋类与动手创作

圆盘拼图、七巧板、图形配对、方块填格和图片拼图，让形状与位置变得直观有趣。还可以描线涂色、摘果子分果子，或下一盘五子棋、中国象棋。

Puzzles, board games and creative activities

Explore shapes and positions through circular puzzles, tangrams, shape matching, block placement and picture puzzles. Trace and colour, pick and sort fruit, or enjoy Gomoku and Chinese chess.

英语、日语与古诗欣赏

在「英语每日一句」和「日语每日一句」中，听日常用语、看中文解释，愿意时跟着读一读，把喜欢的句子留作复习。还可以听古诗朗读、欣赏诗意画面，或玩成语小拼图，在熟悉与新鲜的语言中保持学习的兴趣。

English, Japanese and Chinese poetry

Listen to everyday expressions in English and Japanese, read their Chinese meanings, practise saying them if you wish, and save favourite phrases for review. Enjoy Chinese poetry readings, illustrated scenes and idiom puzzles, exploring both familiar and new language.

四季生活与传统节日

走进《四季小镇》，体验春、夏、秋、冬的生活场景：选春装、看天气、赏花、布置秋收画卷、听街坊拜年。在画面、故事与互动中认识季节变化，重温熟悉的节日习俗。

Seasonal life and traditional festivals

Explore spring, summer, autumn and winter in Four Seasons Town. Choose spring clothing, explore weather and flowers, arrange a harvest scene and listen to neighbours’ New Year greetings. Discover seasonal changes and familiar festival traditions through pictures, stories and activities.

食材认识与家常食谱

从菜篮子里的食材，到夏日家常食谱，看看一道菜需要什么、可以怎样准备。通过食材图片、步骤文字和配套朗读，了解熟悉的家常做法，也为和家人聊聊「今天吃什么」提供话题。

Ingredients and home-style recipes

Explore ingredients in the grocery basket and browse summer home-style recipes. Ingredient pictures, written steps and accompanying readings introduce familiar dishes and offer ideas for family conversations about what to cook.

作息打卡与成长记录 Daily routines and participation records

在「作息打卡」中查看安排、记下已完成的事；在「我的脑力训练成长记录」中回顾累计参与天数、收听内容、收听次数和已记录的活动。听力练习还可查看有记录的听题次数，看看自己听过什么、参与过什么。记录保存在本机，反映参与情况，不是听力检查、认知测评或能力提升证明。

View plans and mark completed tasks in Daily Routines. Review participation days, listening history, listening counts and recorded activities in My Activity Journey. Listening activities also show recorded prompt-listening counts, helping you look back on what you heard and explored. Records stay on the device and reflect participation; they are not hearing tests, cognitive assessments or evidence of improved ability.

看得清楚，用得从容 Clear to read, comfortable to use

清晰的文字、醒目的按钮、熟悉的生活画面与温和反馈，让使用者按自己的兴趣和节奏参与。Android 离线版内置活动页面与配套音频，安装完成后，无网络时也能使用内置内容。

Readable text, clear buttons, familiar scenes and gentle feedback help users take part at their own pace. The offline Android edition includes activity pages and accompanying audio, so built-in content can be used without an internet connection after installation.

Android 应用目前处于 Google Play 封闭测试准备阶段，尚未公开发布。

The Android app is being prepared for closed testing on Google Play and has not been publicly released.

《慢慢来》用于休闲、教育与日常陪伴，不是医疗器械，不用于诊断、治疗或预防认知障碍及其他疾病。

Manmanlai is intended for recreation, education and everyday companionship. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent cognitive conditions or other diseases.
